BODY RUNNER

The dino game, but you are the controller.

JUMP in real life to jump. SQUAT / DUCK to slide under barriers.
Stand back so your whole body is in frame.

Keyboard works too: SPACE = jump, ↓ = duck.

On iPad: open this from your GitHub Pages link (camera needs https),
then Add to Home Screen and play sideways.
